About FPA

Fountainhead Preschool Adajan

Fountainhead Preschool Adajan (FPA) brings the renowned Fountainhead early years experience to the heart of Adajan, Surat. With the same IB PYP-aligned philosophy that underpins FSK and all FS Group campuses, FPA is the ideal starting point for a child's lifelong love of learning. At FPA, every child's curiosity is celebrated. Our warm, nurturing environment is designed to help young learners discover who they are, how they express themselves, and how the world works — through inquiry, play, and collaboration.

Programme

IB PYP Early Years

""

IB Primary Years Programme-aligned early childhood education. Four pillars: Learner Agency, Inquiry-Based Play, Social-Emotional Resilience, and the Environment as Third Teacher.

Mornings begin with an inquiry provocation — a question, an object, a problem to explore. Children move through planned and child-led activities, spending time outdoors, in the arts corner, in the library, and in conversation with each other and their teachers. No two days look exactly alike — and that's the point.

The PAE Process

P

Pre-Admission

Visit FPA, meet the teachers, and experience the warm Fountainhead environment firsthand. Ask every question you have.

A

Assessment

A gentle, play-based interaction to understand your child and answer all your questions. Relaxed, friendly, and child-centred.

E

Enrolment

Welcome to FPA — documentation, orientation day, and your child's first steps at Fountainhead.
