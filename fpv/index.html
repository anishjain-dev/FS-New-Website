About FPV

Fountainhead Preschool Vesu

Fountainhead Preschool Vesu (FPV) is the Fountainhead early years experience for families in the Vesu area of Surat. Backed by the same IB PYP philosophy and the Fountainhead learning model, FPV creates a warm, safe, and intellectually stimulating space for children aged 3–6. At FPV, children are not just cared for — they are inspired. Every day is an inquiry. Every moment is an opportunity to grow, explore, and discover who they are and how the world around them works. Previously The Village Early Learning Centre.
