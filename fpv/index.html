About FPV

Fountainhead Preschool Vesu

Fountainhead Preschool Vesu (FPV) is the Fountainhead early years experience for families in the Vesu area of Surat. Backed by the same IB PYP philosophy and the Fountainhead learning model, FPV creates a warm, safe, and intellectually stimulating space for children aged 3–6.

At FPV, children are not just cared for — they are inspired. Every day is an inquiry. Every moment is an opportunity to grow, explore, and discover who they are and how the world around them works.

Safeguarding Policy

Child Protection Policy

The safety and wellbeing of every child at Fountainhead Preschool Vesu is our highest priority. We are committed to creating a safe, secure, and nurturing environment where every child can flourish.

Key Commitments

  • All staff undergo mandatory Child Protection training
  • A designated Child Protection Officer (CPO) is available at all times
  • Any concerns about a child's welfare are reported immediately and investigated
  • We follow POCSO (Protection of Children from Sexual Offences) Act guidelines
  • All allegations are treated seriously, impartially, and confidentially

Reporting Concerns

If you have any concerns about a child's safety, please contact us at vesu@fountainheadschools.org immediately. All concerns will be acted upon within 24 hours.

Academic Policy

Academic Integrity Policy

FPV values authentic, honest learning. We celebrate original thinking and genuine effort. Every child's ideas and creations are their own — and we protect that from the very beginning.

Key Principles

  • Original effort and creativity are celebrated at all stages
  • Children learn to share ideas openly and acknowledge others' contributions
  • Teachers guide collaborative activities to ensure genuine participation

Language Policy

Language is at the heart of all learning at FPV. We celebrate multilingualism and believe that supporting a child's home language supports their overall development and identity.

Language of Instruction

  • English is the primary language of instruction
  • Mother tongue language support is provided and celebrated
  • Children are encouraged to maintain their home language
  • EAL (English as an Additional Language) support is available
Administrative Policy

Admission Policy

FPV admits children through a welcoming, transparent process. We welcome learners from all backgrounds aged 3—6 years.

Eligibility

  • Children must be aged 3 years or above at the time of admission
  • A warm, play-based interaction is part of the process
  • We welcome children from all backgrounds and communities

Documents Required

  • Birth certificate / Age proof
  • Passport-size photographs
  • Residential address proof
  • Aadhar card (child and parents)
Health Policy

Health Exclusion Policy

To protect the health of all children and staff, FPV follows a Health Exclusion Policy specifying when children should be kept home and when they may return.

When to Keep Your Child at Home

  • Fever (above 100—F / 37.8—C) — child must be fever-free for 24 hours before returning
  • Vomiting or diarrhoea — must be symptom-free for 24 hours
  • Chickenpox — until all blisters have crusted over
  • Pink eye (Conjunctivitis) — until treatment has begun and discharge has stopped
Partnership Policy

Parent Role in Education

FPV believes that the most powerful learning happens when home and school work as one. Parents are our most valued partners in learning.

How Parents Can Help

  • Attend orientation sessions to understand the FPV approach
  • Ask open questions at home: "What did you wonder about today?"
  • Read aloud to your child every day
  • Foster independence — let children struggle productively before jumping in
  • Attend Parent-Teacher meetings and school events
  • Share your own curiosity and love of learning with your child
