Fountainhead School Kunkni Ranks 5th Nationally — Cfore 2025

← Back to News & Happenings

Fountainhead School Kunkni, Surat has been ranked 5th nationally in the Cfore School Rankings 2025 — a prestigious independent assessment of schools across India. This recognition places FSK among the top-performing schools in the country, affirming the quality of our pedagogy, curriculum, and holistic education approach.

The Cfore School Rankings evaluate institutions across multiple dimensions including Pedagogy & Curriculum, Faculty Excellence, Infrastructure, Academic Performance, and Student Holistic Development. FSK's performance in Pedagogy & Curriculum was particularly notable, scoring at the highest levels in this category.

This achievement reflects the sustained commitment of our teachers, students, and families to building a school culture centred on inquiry, agency, and genuine learning. We are proud to be among India's finest schools and continue our journey forward.

About Cfore Rankings

The Cfore School Rankings are one of India's most respected independent school evaluation frameworks, assessing schools across parameters including Pedagogy & Curriculum, Infrastructure, Faculty, Academic Performance, and Holistic Development.
