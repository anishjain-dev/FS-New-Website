Fountainhead School Honoured at the IDA Awards 2025

← Back to News & Happenings

Fountainhead School has been recognised at the IDA Awards 2025 for its exemplary work in wellbeing, engagement, and capacity building in the foundational years. This recognition is especially meaningful to us because Social and Emotional Learning (SEL) has been an integral part of our philosophy long before it became a widespread priority in education. Over the years, our approach has grown through the consistent efforts of our wellbeing counselors, teachers, parents, and school leadership.

At the centre of this work is our dedicated wellbeing team comprising seven trained counsellors who form the backbone of our emotional support system. Each student receives two wellbeing check-ins every year, enabling early identification of emotional concerns or learning delays. Our counselors collaborate closely with teachers and parents to ensure that interventions are timely, sensitive, and tailored to the needs of each child.

Wellbeing at Fountainhead is embedded deeply within our pedagogy. Tools such as Six Bricks help young learners develop resilience and self-regulation. Block Play provides space for quiet focus, imagination, and self-directed exploration. The Leader in Me habits nurture personal responsibility, collaboration, and a sense of accomplishment. Our Life Classes create safe environments for discussions on topics such as body safety, bullying, and gender stereotypes — empowering students with awareness and confidence from an early age.

These practices collectively shape a school culture where children feel secure, supported, and ready to thrive.

We are grateful for this honour and remain committed to building a learning environment where every child feels seen, safe, and heard.
