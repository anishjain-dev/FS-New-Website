Fountainhead Schools — Great Place to Work Certified

← Back to News & Happenings

Fountainhead Schools has been officially certified as a Great Place to Work — a recognition that reflects our commitment to building a workplace where educators feel valued, respected, supported, and inspired to do their best work.

The Great Place to Work certification is based on a rigorous survey of our faculty and staff across all campuses, measuring dimensions including trust, fairness, camaraderie, credibility, and pride. Earning this certification means that our people — our teachers, administrators, and support staff — themselves affirm that Fountainhead is a great place to work.

We believe that a thriving faculty creates thriving students. When teachers are happy, supported in their professional growth, and part of a collaborative community, that energy flows directly into classrooms and into the lives of the children they teach.

We are proud of this certification and deeply grateful to our entire team for making Fountainhead what it is. This award belongs to every person who has contributed to our culture of care, curiosity, and continuous growth.

About Great Place to Work

Great Place to Work is the global authority on workplace culture, measuring employee trust and experience across organisations in 170+ countries. Certification is granted based on anonymous employee survey results.
