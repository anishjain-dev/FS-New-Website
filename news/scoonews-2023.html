ScooNews — Best School for Skill Development 2023

← Back to News & Happenings

Fountainhead School has been recognised by ScooNews as the Best School for Skill Development 2023. This award acknowledges our commitment to going beyond academic excellence — nurturing students who are equipped with practical skills, creative thinking, collaboration, and agency for the real world.

At Fountainhead, skill development is woven into everyday learning. From design thinking workshops and project-based learning to sports, performing arts, and community engagement — every programme is designed with intentionality. Our IB framework creates natural pathways for students to explore, take risks, reflect, and grow across disciplines.

The ScooNews awards celebrate innovation and excellence in Indian education, and this recognition affirms the work of our students, teachers, and leadership in building a school culture that truly prepares young people for life beyond school.

We are grateful and energised by this recognition. Our students' achievements are the greatest reward, and this award is dedicated to each one of them.
