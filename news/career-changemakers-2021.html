Fountainhead School — Career ChangeMakers 2021

← Back to News & Happenings

Fountainhead School has been recognised as a Career ChangeMakers institution in 2021 — an award that celebrates schools that are reshaping how students think about careers, purpose, and their role in the world. This recognition reflects our long-standing commitment to future-readiness and preparing students for careers that may not yet exist.

Our approach to career education goes far beyond traditional guidance. Through our IB programmes, project-based learning, and real-world partnerships, students at Fountainhead regularly engage with professionals, explore entrepreneurship, and develop the skills and mindsets that today's rapidly changing world demands.
