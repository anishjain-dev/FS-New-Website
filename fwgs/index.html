About FWGS

A School With a Vision Forward

Fountainhead Wockhardt Global School (FWGS) is the product of a unique partnership — where the values of an established institution meet the progressive pedagogy of Fountainhead Schools. Today, FWGS is a full IB continuum school offering Early Years, PYP, MYP, and the IB Diploma Programme. The school is committed to nurturing leaders with character and competence — graduates who are globally-minded, locally grounded, and ready for the challenges and opportunities of the 21st century.

Academic Programmes at FWGS

Ages 3—6

Early Years Programme

Four pillars: PYP Learner Agency, Inquiry-Based Play, Social-Emotional Resilience, and Environment as Third Teacher.

Grades 1—5

Primary Years Programme

Six transdisciplinary units of inquiry per year. Collaborative, concept-driven learning through real-world connections.

Grades 6—10

Middle Years Programme

Eight subject groups with global context. Critical thinking, intercultural awareness, and community service as action.

Grades 11—12

IB Diploma Programme

Internationally recognised university preparation. Six subject groups, Theory of Knowledge, Extended Essay, and CAS.

Admissions

Joining FWGS — The FS Way

Our admission process — PAE — is transparent, fair, and centred on the child.

The PAE Process

P

Pre-Admission

Initial enquiry and campus tour. Meet our admissions team to learn about our programmes, facilities, and the IB continuum.

A

Assessment

Age-appropriate interaction. For younger grades, a warm play-based session. For older grades, a casual conversation with our academic team.

E

Enrolment

Offer of admission, documentation, fee payment, and welcome orientation. Your child joins the Fountainhead family.

Documents Required
  • Child & parent photograph (grey background, as on Aadhaar)
  • Family photograph (parents, child & siblings)
  • Child's birth certificate
  • Vaccination card (mandatory till Grade 2)
  • Report cards for previous two grades
  • School undertaking (hard copy)
  • No-due certificate (hard copy; not for EY)
  • International applicants: passport & visa copies
  • Original School Leaving Certificate once admission is confirmed
