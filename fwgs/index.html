About FWGS

A School With a Vision Forward

Fountainhead Wockhardt Global School (FWGS) is the product of a unique partnership � where the values of an established institution meet the progressive pedagogy of Fountainhead Schools. Today, FWGS is a full IB continuum school offering Early Years, PYP, MYP, and the IB Diploma Programme.

The school is committed to nurturing leaders with character and competence � graduates who are globally-minded, locally grounded, and ready for the challenges and opportunities of the 21st century.

Early Years Programme

The EYP at FWGS

The Early Years Programme at FWGS is a carefully designed introduction to learning � rooted in the IB philosophy, driven by inquiry, and centred on the whole child.

The Four Pillars of EYP at FWGS

??

PYP Learner Agency

Children are not passive recipients of knowledge � they are active agents of their own learning. At FWGS, we design experiences that give children genuine choice, voice, and ownership of their learning journey from the very beginning.

??

Inquiry-Based Play

Play is not a break from learning � it is the most powerful form of learning in early childhood. Every activity at FWGS is designed to provoke curiosity, encourage exploration, and develop critical thinking through hands-on, meaningful play.

??

Social-Emotional Resilience

We develop the emotional vocabulary and resilience that children need to navigate relationships, setbacks, and the complexity of the world. Social-emotional learning is woven into every moment of the school day � not treated as a separate subject.

??

Environment as Third Teacher

The classroom itself is a learning tool. We design our spaces intentionally � with materials that invite exploration, natural elements that ground children, and displays that reflect and extend their thinking. The environment teaches alongside the teacher.

Student Policy

International Student Policy

FWGS welcomes students from all over the world. We are committed to providing an inclusive, supportive environment for international students and their families � from the moment of application through enrolment and beyond.

Admissions for International Students

  • International students are welcome to apply for all year groups subject to availability
  • English proficiency assessment may be required; EAL support is available
  • All necessary student visa / documentation guidance is provided by our admissions team
  • Visa documentation support is available for families relocating to India

Support for International Families

  • Welcome orientation and campus tour for new international families
  • EAL (English as an Additional Language) support throughout the school year
  • Multilingual communication where possible; primary language of instruction is English
  • Integration activities to help new students build friendships and feel at home
Safeguarding

Child Protection Policy

The safety and wellbeing of every child is FWGS's highest priority. We follow all IB safeguarding guidelines and comply with POCSO Act requirements. All staff are trained in Child Protection. Concerns are reported immediately and investigated confidentially.

Academic

Academic Integrity Policy

FWGS is committed to academic integrity across all IB programmes. All submitted work must be original, properly cited, and authentically the student's own. Malpractice is investigated and consequences applied as per IB and school guidelines.

Academic

Assessment Policy

Assessment at FWGS is varied, meaningful, and growth-oriented. We use formative and summative assessment across all programmes. Progress is reported termly through the Nucleus portal and communicated at Parent-Teacher Conferences.
