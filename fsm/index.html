Safeguarding Policy

Child Protection Policy

The safety and wellbeing of every child at Fountainhead School Malgama is our highest priority. We are committed to creating a safe, secure, and nurturing environment where every child can flourish.

Key Commitments

  • All staff undergo mandatory Child Protection training
  • A designated Child Protection Officer (CPO) is available at all times
  • Any concerns about a child's welfare are reported immediately and investigated
  • We follow POCSO (Protection of Children from Sexual Offences) Act guidelines
  • All allegations are treated seriously, impartially, and confidentially

Reporting Concerns

If you have any concerns about a child's safety, please contact the school's Child Protection Officer directly or email surat@fountainheadschools.org immediately. All concerns will be acted upon within 24 hours.

Academic Policy

Academic Integrity Policy

Fountainhead School Malgama is committed to academic integrity — the honest, authentic, and responsible production of work. Academic integrity is a fundamental value of an IB education.

Key Principles

  • All submitted work must be the student's own authentic work
  • Proper citation and referencing of all sources is mandatory
  • Collaboration is encouraged but must be clearly distinguished from collusion
  • Plagiarism, malpractice, and misconduct will be investigated and addressed
Academic Policy

Language Policy

Language is central to all learning at FSM. Our Language Policy reflects our commitment to multilingualism and the IB's belief that all teachers are language teachers.

Language of Instruction

  • English is the primary language of instruction across all programmes
  • Mother tongue language support is provided and celebrated
  • Students are encouraged to maintain and develop their home language
  • All teachers are responsible for developing academic language in their subject area
Administrative Policy

Admission Policy

FSM admits students through a transparent, fair, and holistic process. We welcome learners from all backgrounds who demonstrate curiosity, enthusiasm, and a willingness to embrace the IB learner profile.

Eligibility

  • Students must meet the age criteria for the relevant grade
  • Previous academic records and school reports are reviewed
  • An interaction or assessment may be scheduled for the student
Student Policy

Uniform Policy

FSM believes that a uniform fosters a sense of belonging, equality, and school pride. All students are required to wear the prescribed FSM uniform on all school days and at school events unless otherwise notified.

General Guidelines

  • Complete, clean, and neat uniform is mandatory every school day
  • Sports uniform to be worn on PE days only
  • Name tags must be affixed to all uniform items
Academic Policy

No Tuition Policy

Fountainhead School strongly discourages students from taking private tuitions from former or current Fountainhead teachers. The school believes that its own instruction is sufficient for students to achieve their academic goals.

Important Clarification

Coaching for competitive examinations such as NEET, JEE, SAT, etc. is NOT discouraged. Only academic tuitions from former or current FS teachers are restricted under this policy.

Health Policy

Health Exclusion Policy

To protect the health of all students and staff, FSM follows a Health Exclusion Policy that specifies when students should be kept home and when they may return to school.

When to Keep Your Child at Home

  • Fever (above 100—F / 37.8—C) — child must be fever-free for 24 hours before returning
  • Vomiting or diarrhoea — must be symptom-free for 24 hours
  • Chickenpox — until all blisters have crusted over
  • Pink eye (Conjunctivitis) — until treatment has begun and discharge has stopped
Partnership Policy

Parent Role in Education

FSM believes that a child's education is best when school and parents work in genuine partnership. This policy outlines what we expect from parents as active participants in their child's learning.

School Activities & Expectations

  • Attend all Parent Orientation sessions
  • Attend Parent-Teacher Conferences (PTCs) and Student-Led Conferences (SLCs)
  • Participate in school events and volunteer for activities
  • Support your child to do self-study — not do it for them
  • Foster independence in learning habits and daily routine
