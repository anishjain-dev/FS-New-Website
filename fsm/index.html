About FSM

Fountainhead School Malgama

Fountainhead School Malgama (FSM) is the newest addition to the Fountainhead Schools group — bringing the same outstanding IB education that Surat knows and trusts, to a brand new campus location. Offering Early Years and the IB Primary Years Programme (PYP), FSM will be a warm, vibrant, and modern learning environment for children aged 3 to 11. Ground breaking has been completed — FSM is on its way.

Programmes

Learning at FSM

Ages 3—6

Early Years Programme

Play-based inquiry rooted in the IB PYP framework. Learner agency, social-emotional resilience, the environment as the third teacher. Every child is seen, heard, and celebrated.

Grades 1—5

Primary Years Programme (PYP)

Six transdisciplinary units of inquiry per year. Collaborative, concept-driven learning developing the whole child and the IB learner profile through authentic, meaningful experiences.

The PAE Process

P

Pre-Admission

Reach out to our admissions team to register your interest and receive updates about FSM's opening timeline. Visit the site or connect with us online.

A

Assessment

Age-appropriate interaction to understand your child — warm, play-based, and focused on curiosity rather than academic performance.

E

Enrolment

Welcome to the FSM family — documentation, orientation, and your child's exciting beginning at Fountainhead.

Documents Required
  • Birth certificate / Age proof
  • Previous school's Transfer Certificate (TC)
  • Last two years' academic report cards
  • Passport-size photographs (4 copies)
  • Residential address proof
  • Aadhar card (child and parents)
