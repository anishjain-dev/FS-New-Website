About FSM

Fountainhead School Malgama

Fountainhead School Malgama (FSM) is the newest addition to the Fountainhead Schools group — bringing the same outstanding IB education that Surat knows and trusts, to a brand new campus location.

Offering Early Years and the IB Primary Years Programme (PYP), FSM will be a warm, vibrant, and modern learning environment for children aged 3 to 11. Ground breaking has been completed — FSM is on its way.

Milestone

Ground Breaking Ceremony

The official Ground Breaking Ceremony for FSM has been completed, marking the beginning of construction on the new campus.

Programmes

Learning at FSM

Ages 3–6

Early Years Programme

Play-based inquiry rooted in the IB PYP framework. Learner agency, social-emotional resilience, the environment as the third teacher. Every child is seen, heard, and celebrated.

Grades 1–5

Primary Years Programme (PYP)

Six transdisciplinary units of inquiry per year. Collaborative, concept-driven learning developing the whole child and the IB learner profile through authentic, meaningful experiences.

Learning Philosophy

The FS Learning Model at FSM

FSM is built on the same pedagogical foundation as FSK — the flagship. Students are at the centre of everything we do. We place a strong emphasis on fostering a love of learning, building academic competencies alongside physical fitness, creativity, and social-emotional wellbeing.
