About FALH

Fountainhead Avadh Learning Hub

The Fountainhead Avadh Learning Hub (FALH) is something new and exciting — a dual-purpose educational space in Vapi, Gujarat. FALH combines a warm, IB-aligned preschool for young learners aged 3–6 with a vibrant after-school enrichment hub for older children.

Built on the same Fountainhead learning philosophy that powers India's 4th ranked school, FALH brings exceptional early years and enrichment education to the Vapi community — close to home, and connected to the world.

What We Offer at FALH

Preschool · Ages 3–6

IB-Aligned Preschool

A warm, play-based preschool rooted in the IB learning philosophy. Every child is a curious, capable learner from the very first day.

  • Inquiry-based learning through play
  • Learner agency and social-emotional development
  • Environment as the third teacher
  • Small class sizes for personalised attention

An enrichment hub for school-age children — structured activities, guided learning, and creative exploration after school hours.

  • Homework support and academic enrichment
  • Arts, music, movement
  • STEM and creative thinking activities
  • Social-emotional learning and wellbeing

Safeguarding Policy

Child Protection Policy

The safety and wellbeing of every child at Fountainhead Avadh Learning Hub is our highest priority. We are committed to creating a safe, secure, and nurturing environment where every child can flourish.

Key Commitments

  • All staff undergo mandatory Child Protection training
  • A designated Child Protection Officer (CPO) is available at all times
  • Any concerns about a child's welfare are reported immediately and investigated
  • We follow POCSO (Protection of Children from Sexual Offences) Act guidelines
  • All allegations are treated seriously, impartially, and confidentially

Reporting Concerns

If you have any concerns about a child's safety, please contact us at vapi@fountainheadschools.org immediately. All concerns will be acted upon within 24 hours.

Academic Policy

Academic Integrity Policy

FALH is committed to honest, authentic learning. All work must be the student's own genuine effort. We value the process of learning as much as the outcome, and we encourage children to take pride in their own thinking and creativity.

Key Principles

  • Original, authentic effort is celebrated at all stages
  • Students learn to attribute ideas and work to their sources
  • Collaboration is guided by teachers to develop learning, not replace it
Administrative Policy

Admission Policy

FALH admits children through a welcoming, transparent process. We welcome learners from all backgrounds who are ready to grow, explore, and be part of our vibrant learning community.

Eligibility

  • Preschool: Children aged 3–6 years
  • After-School Hub: School-age children of relevant age
  • An informal interaction with the child is part of the process

Documents Required

  • Birth certificate / Age proof
  • Passport-size photographs
  • Residential address proof
  • Aadhar card (child and parents)
Health Policy

Health Exclusion Policy

To protect the health of all children and staff, FALH follows a Health Exclusion Policy specifying when children should be kept home and when they may return.

When to Keep Your Child at Home

  • Fever (above 100°F / 37.8°C) — child must be fever-free for 24 hours before returning
  • Vomiting or diarrhoea — must be symptom-free for 24 hours
  • Chickenpox — until all blisters have crusted over
  • Pink eye (Conjunctivitis) — until treatment has begun and discharge has stopped
Partnership Policy

Parent Role in Education

FALH believes that a child's learning is best when home and school work together. Parents are our strongest partners.

How Parents Can Help

  • Attend orientation sessions to understand the FALH learning approach
  • Maintain regular communication with teachers
  • Encourage your child's curiosity and questions at home
  • Support your child's independence — guide, don't do it for them
  • Foster a reading culture and love of learning at home
