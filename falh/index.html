About FALH

Fountainhead Avadh Learning Hub

The Fountainhead Avadh Learning Hub (FALH) is something new and exciting — a dual-purpose educational space in Vapi, Gujarat. FALH combines a warm, IB-aligned preschool for young learners aged 3–6 with a vibrant after-school enrichment hub for older children.

Built on the same Fountainhead learning philosophy that powers India's 4th ranked school, FALH brings exceptional early years and enrichment education to the Vapi community — close to home, and connected to the world.

Programmes

What We Offer at FALH

Preschool · Ages 3–6

IB-Aligned Preschool

A warm, play-based preschool rooted in the IB learning philosophy. Every child is a curious, capable learner from the very first day.

  • Inquiry-based learning through play
  • Learner agency and social-emotional development
  • Environment as the third teacher
  • Small class sizes for personalised attention
After-School Hub

After-School Enrichment

An enrichment hub for school-age children — structured activities, guided learning, and creative exploration after school hours.

  • Homework support and academic enrichment
  • Arts, music, movement
  • STEM and creative thinking activities
  • Social-emotional learning and wellbeing
