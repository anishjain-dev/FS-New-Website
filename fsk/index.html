About FSK

A School That Believes Learning is a Lifelong Joy

At Fountainhead School Kunkni, we believe that learning is a lifelong process. We aim to inculcate a sheer sense of joy in the learning process among students, parents, and teachers alike.

Our goal is to provide a happy, purposeful, safe, and nurturing environment that equips our children with the skills, attitudes, and character needed for sustainable success in life.

We place a strong emphasis on fostering a love of learning. Our pedagogy is learner-centred and creates a positive and supportive environment where students are at the centre of everything we do.

Academics

The IB Continuum at FSK

From early years through Diploma, our programmes develop inquiring, knowledgeable, and caring young people.

Primary Years Programme

Inquiry-Led Learning from the Very Beginning

The PYP offers a transdisciplinary framework that nurtures curiosity and a love of learning in children aged 3–12. Students develop the knowledge, skills, attitudes, and understanding they need to be active participants in a lifelong journey of learning.

Our PYP programme focuses on developing inquirers, thinkers, communicators, and principled learners who are open-minded, caring, and reflective.

Middle Years Programme

Building Bridges Between Learning and Real Life

The MYP is a challenging framework that encourages students to make practical connections between their studies and the real world. It develops confident, independent, reflective learners who are skilled at researching, thinking critically, and communicating effectively.

Students complete a Community Project (Grade 9) and Personal Project (Grade 10), developing self-management, research, and social skills.

Diploma Programme

College-Ready. World-Ready.

The IB Diploma Programme is an internationally recognised qualification for students aged 16–19. It provides a rigorous academic challenge, developing students' intellectual, personal, emotional, and social skills.

DP students choose from six subject groups, complete Theory of Knowledge (TOK), an Extended Essay, and Creativity, Activity, Service (CAS) — developing well-rounded graduates ready for top universities worldwide.
